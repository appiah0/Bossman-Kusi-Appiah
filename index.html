The Athlete

Built to Clear
Every Hurdle

Bossman Kusi Appiah is a powerhouse in the making — a Ghanaian track and field sensation who has turned the 400m hurdles into his personal stage. Representing Prempeh College in the Ashanti Region, Bossman has shown the speed, stamina, and technique that are the hallmarks of a future champion.

In the 2025/26 Ashanti Region Schools & Colleges Athletics Super Zonal championships, Bossman did not just win — he shattered the regional record, clocking an astonishing 51.69 seconds to claim gold. At the 2026 National Open Championships in Cape Coast, he won gold in 52.34s — earning qualification to the 2026 World U20 Athletics Championships in Eugene, Oregon, USA.

A three-time consecutive Prempeh College champion (Form 1, 2 and 3), Bossman's dominance is no accident. With elite footwork over 10 hurdles, controlled aggression in the back stretch, and a finishing kick that few can match, he is a name that Ghanaian athletics — and the world — will be hearing for years to come.

The Science

Elite Technique

The 400m hurdles demands a unique blend of explosive speed, endurance, and flawless hurdling mechanics over 10 barriers in 400 metres.

⚡
Explosive Start
A powerful drive phase out of the blocks sets the tone for the entire race. The first 45m to Hurdle 1 is where rhythm is established. Bossman's fast-twitch explosiveness gives him an early edge.
01
🏃
Stride Pattern
Elite 400m hurdlers maintain a locked stride pattern between hurdles — typically 13–15 strides. Consistency in stride frequency and length is what separates champions from competitors.
02
🦵
Hurdle Clearance
Clearing each 91.4cm barrier with minimal air time is critical. A flat, attack-style lead leg combined with a fast trail leg keeps forward momentum and prevents time loss over each hurdle.
03
💨
Back Stretch Drive
The 200–300m segment is where races are won or lost. Maintaining form under fatigue, managing lactic acid buildup, and staying mechanically sound through hurdles 5–8 is essential.
04
🔥
Final Sprint
The last 40m after the 10th hurdle is a pure sprint to the line. Athletes who preserve their form through the final hurdle and maintain arm drive in the home straight claim the top spot.
05
🧠
Race Intelligence
Knowing when to push, when to conserve, and how to adapt when a hurdle disrupts rhythm is a mental skill. Bossman's composure under pressure is a defining trait of his athletic identity.
06
